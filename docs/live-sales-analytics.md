# Live Sales Analytics & Real-Time Dashboard

## Real-Time Live Session Dashboard

Accessible at `/space/[workspaceId]/live-sales`:

### 1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- **Realized Revenue**: Completed and paid orders.
- **Potential Revenue**: Reserved orders awaiting payment.
- **Conversion Rate %**: Ratio of paid orders vs. total reserved orders.
- **Average Order Value (AOV)**: Average spend per customer.

### 2. Best-Selling SKU Leaderboard
- Top 8 highest-volume products during the broadcast.
- Visual percentage bars and quantity counts.

### 3. Payment Method & Hourly Timeline
- Breakdown between Bank Transfer (Easyslip) and Credit Card (Moneyspace).
- Hourly sales velocity chart showing peak selling moments during the live stream.

## AI Cart Recovery Urgency Reminders

Pending orders nearing the 24-hour expiration threshold can be recovered with 1 click:
- Generates high-converting Thai reminder copy tailored to the live session.
- Automatically inserts the customer's checkout link and expiration countdown.
- Dispatches reminders directly via Messenger DM or LINE OA.
